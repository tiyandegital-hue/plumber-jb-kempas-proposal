What this demo collects

Almost nothing. The interactive chat demo runs entirely in your browser — your taps, typed name and chosen options are never sent anywhere and are never stored. The contact form on this demo page does not transmit data.

What the live system would collect

If this proposal becomes a real system on the business's WhatsApp number, it would process:

  • Messages you send to the business WhatsApp (to answer and quote) — handled under WhatsApp's and Meta's terms.
