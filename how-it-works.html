The pipeline

Five nodes.
One job, closed.

Every node runs on your rules — your services, your areas, your prices, your calendar. The AI executes; it never improvises.

NODE 01

Customer photo

Leak, clog, heater — arrives on your WhatsApp, the channel JB customers already message on.

NODE 02

AI triage

Identifies the job type and asks the two questions you'd ask — area and urgency. Tricky cases go straight to you.

NODE 03

Instant quote

A range pulled from your price list in ~60 seconds. Final price always confirmed on-site — stated openly.

NODE 04

Booking

Slot offered from your real availability. Customer picks, job lands in your calendar with the address attached.

NODE 05

Reminder + review

Automatic "we're on the way" before the visit, and a review ask after — feeding the 4.8★ you already earned.

Under the hood

A pipeline, not a magic trick

Built like proper software: intake (WhatsApp Business API) → vision triage (classifies the photo into your job types) → quote engine (your ranges, your rules) → booking (your calendar) → follow-up (reminders + review asks). Every step is logged, every reply follows your script, and you can read or override anything.

GUARDRAIL 01

Your prices or nothing

The bot can only quote ranges you approve. No range on file → it asks you, not the customer.

GUARDRAIL 02

You stay in charge

Blocked slots stay blocked. Emergency keywords (gas, flooding) escalate to your phone immediately.
